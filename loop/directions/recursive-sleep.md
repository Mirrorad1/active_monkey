# direction: recursive-sleep

**Question.** Can an evidence-preserving sleep lifecycle improve the lab's use of previous
research, and can the same lifecycle improve a creature's learning from its lived experience?

**Authorization.** Human approved the two-level design on 2026-10-27:
`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-27-recursive-sleep-design.md`.

**Why it matters.** The lab already archives results; the creature already learns online.
Neither establishes a benefit from offline consolidation. Test that benefit against ordinary
retrieval/replay at disclosed data, capacity and computational budgets before adding ecology.

**Experiment ladder.**
1. L0: compile/review source-linked lab briefs; sources, negative verdicts and caveats survive.
   FAIL: unsupported knowledge, omitted negatives, stale review, or context-budget overrun.
2. C0: once-weighted joint refitting vs chronological refitting and idle on the short dyad
   history. FAIL: either preregistered mean-effect or seed-count requirement fails. Exp 279
   defines the exact instrument; no adding replayed events as independent observations.
3. C1, conditional on C0: old-task retention, interference and changed-world revision. FAIL:
   overfits replay, forgets prior knowledge, or cannot revise. New preregistration required.
4. C2/C3, conditional on useful learning: imposed sleep at time/energy/memory costs, then
   invasion-from-rarity of heritable capacity. FAIL: no net benefit, drift, unstable substrate,
   or accidental inheritance of acquired knowledge. Existing ecology preflight binds.
5. L1: prospective, blinded, budget-matched research-design comparison. FAIL: no improvement
   in declared design-quality metrics. Positive experiment rate is not a success metric.

**Boundaries.** Lab memory is persistent artifacts, not updates to hosted-model weights.
Creature replay updates its own numerical model. Research design remains conductor-owned;
`meta_monkey` stays advisory; current `PROTOCOL`/`VALIDATION`/`ROUTING` bind. The lifecycle
does not automatically select studies or rewrite truth. Generated rehearsal is labeled and
cannot become observed data. Natural selection remains individual births/deaths; offspring
inherit capacity/priors, not a parent's acquired beliefs, absent a separately named treatment.

**Stop condition.** A failed falsifier halts that experimental rung. Log negative evidence
with the same source fidelity as a positive. Lab-instrument correctness, creature-learning
benefit, and evolved sleep are independently evaluated. No extrapolation between those claims.
